Our Journey

SSB Poultry started out as a small family backyard operation raising broilers for neighbours and local spaza shops in Emalahleni, Mpumalanga. Through dedication, strict hygiene standards, and strong community relationships, we have expanded into a full-scale regional supplier.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality poultry products and agricultural inputs to local households, small businesses, and emerging poultry farmers.

Our Vision

To become Emalahleni’s most trusted and sustainable hub for poultry farming and fresh produce.
